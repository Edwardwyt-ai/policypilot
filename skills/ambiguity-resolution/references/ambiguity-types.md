# Ambiguity Types

- **Object:** more than one target entity fits.
- **Intent:** informational and transactional readings differ materially.
- **Constraint:** threshold, firmness, exception, or trade-off is unclear.
- **Reference:** pronoun or shorthand has several antecedents.
- **Identity:** supplied identifier is incomplete or conflicts with target ownership.
- **Temporal:** current, historical, effective-date, or deadline scope is unclear.

Mark ambiguity blocking only when interpretations lead to different required evidence or actions.
