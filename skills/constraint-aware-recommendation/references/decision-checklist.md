# Decision Checklist

- Every selected candidate satisfies all evidenced hard constraints.
- No unknown critical criterion was treated as true.
- Candidate identity and evidence scope match.
- Preferences precede optimization only after eligibility filtering.
- Exclusions cite a material criterion, not missing optional data.
- The answer states key fit and permitted trade-offs without hidden reasoning.
