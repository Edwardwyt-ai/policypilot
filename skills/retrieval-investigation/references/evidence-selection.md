# Evidence Selection

Prefer passages whose entity, aspect, condition, and scope match the active Need. Preserve document provenance and enough local context to interpret exceptions or conjunctions.

Do not promote a document merely because it ranks highly. Exclude duplicates and adjacent material that does not change coverage. Decision context should receive compact Evidence, not every candidate document.
